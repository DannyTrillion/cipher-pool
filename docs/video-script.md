# 3-minute video script (real-person pitch, screen recording)

**0:00–0:25 · On camera.** "Hi, I'm [name]. This is Cipher Pool: PoolTogether, except nobody, not even the contract, can see your balance. Deposit, keep your principal, and each round the yield goes to one saver. Everything is encrypted with Zama's FHEVM. Let me show you."

**0:25–0:50 · Screen: home.** Point at the public prize, countdown, saver count. Connect wallet. Click "Get 1,000 test cUSD". Show wallet balance appears as a cipher strip. Click "Reveal balances", sign once, values resolve.

**0:50–1:20 · Deposit.** Deposit 400. Narrate the staged status: one-time operator approval, encrypting locally, confirming. Reveal: pool balance 400. Open Etherscan tab briefly: the deposit event carries a ciphertext handle, no amount.

**1:20–1:45 · Second wallet + sponsor.** Switch account, deposit 100. Sponsor 25 cUSD to the prize. Hero prize updates. Mention eligibility note under the position card.

**1:45–2:25 · The draw.** Countdown hits zero, click "Run draw". Narrate: harvest yield, draw FHE seed, batch 1 selecting over encrypted balances, batch 2 crediting. Progress bar. Draw complete. Click "Did I win?" on both accounts: one sees a prize, the other sees "not this time". Show "Publish proof of win".

**2:25–2:45 · Verify.** Draws page: seed and prize are public, winner is not. Etherscan: every participant was touched in the same tx.

**2:45–3:00 · On camera.** "Withdraw any time, principal untouched. Swap the mock yield source for a real vault and this is ready for a cUSDT mainnet pool. Code and demo are linked below."
