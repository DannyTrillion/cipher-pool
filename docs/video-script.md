# 3-minute video script (real-person pitch, screen recording)

**0:00–0:25 · On camera.** "Hi, I'm [name]. This is Cipher Pool: PoolTogether, except nobody, not even the contract, can see your balance. Deposit, keep your principal, and each round the yield goes to one saver. Everything is encrypted with Zama's FHEVM. Let me show you."

**0:25–0:55 · Screen: home.** Point at the public prize, the big button's countdown, saver count. Connect wallet with "New here" on. Step 2: "Get 1,000 test USDT" (a plain ERC-20). Step 3: "Shield into cUSDT" — say: approve, then wrap; from here every amount is encrypted. Show cUSDT in wallet appearing as a cipher strip.

**0:55–1:20 · Deposit.** Step 4: deposit 300. Narrate: one-time operator approval, encrypting locally, confirming. Step 5: "Unlock my numbers", sign once (EIP-712), savings resolve to 300. Open Etherscan briefly: the deposit event carries a ciphertext handle, no amount.

**1:20–1:45 · Second wallet + sponsor.** Switch account, deposit 100. Sponsor 25 cUSDT to the prize. Hero prize updates. Mention eligibility note under the position card.

**1:45–2:30 · The draw + claim.** Countdown hits zero: hold the big button one second. Narrate: yield drips in, five FHE seeds, pass 1 selecting over encrypted balances, pass 2 crediting encrypted pots. Sphere sweeps, then bursts. Click "Did I win?": reels spin and land; one account wins. Click "Claim to wallet" — a confidential transfer; say anyone can call claim, losers move an encrypted zero, so claiming reveals nothing.

**2:30–2:45 · Verify + withdraw.** Draws page: seeds and prize are public, winner is not. Back on Play, Experienced tab: withdraw principal — it comes back as cUSDT, untouched.

**2:45–3:00 · On camera.** "Every balance, weight, ticket and prize stays encrypted; only the seeds and the prize are public, so anyone can verify a draw. Swap the prize drip for a real vault adapter and this is ready for a cUSDT mainnet pool. Code and demo are linked below."
